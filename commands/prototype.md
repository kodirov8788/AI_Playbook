# Command: prototype

## Trigger
Invoked via `/prototype` or `prototype`.

## Actions
1. Design a clean, minimal self-contained MVP for rapid experimental testing.
2. Outline implementation steps and sandbox safety boundaries.
3. Output details formatted exactly as:
   - MVP: `<what is the isolated feature>`
   - Files: `<paths to sandbox files>`
   - Steps: `<step-by-step layout of prototype implementation>`
   - Risk: `<low/med/high with explanation>`
   - Rollback: `<instructions to purge prototype and restore master branches>`
